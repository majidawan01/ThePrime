Virtual Assistants for Real Estate Agents

Never miss a buyer or seller inquiry. We answer calls, book showings, and manage leads while you close deals.

You Can't Show Properties and Answer Calls at the Same Time

As a real estate agent, your phone rings when you're in showings, at closings, or with clients. Every missed call is a potential buyer or seller going to your competitor. ThePrime ensures every inquiry is answered professionally—so you never lose a deal to voicemail.

How We Support Real Estate Professionals

Answer Every Call

We answer in your business name, screen calls, and forward urgent inquiries immediately.

Result: No more missed buyer/seller leads. Your phone is always covered.

Schedule Showings & Appointments

We coordinate property showings, listing appointments, and consultations directly with your calendar.

Result: Your calendar stays full without the scheduling back-and-forth.

Qualify & Follow Up with Leads

We ask qualifying questions, log details in your CRM, and follow up until they're ready to move forward.

Result: Only serious, pre-qualified leads reach your phone.

Manage Open House Inquiries

We handle post-open house follow-up calls, answer questions, and schedule private showings.

Result: Turn open house visitors into booked appointments.

CRM & Database Management

We log every interaction, update contact info, and keep your database organized.

Result: Clean data and no manual entry work for you.

Transaction Coordination Support

We help coordinate inspections, appraisals, and closing appointments.

Result: Smoother transactions with less stress.
