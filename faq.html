General Questions

Our virtual assistants handle the day-to-day operations that keep your business running but take you away from revenue-generating activities. This includes answering calls, scheduling appointments, managing your calendar, following up with leads, entering data into your CRM, and handling administrative tasks. Think of us as your remote support team.
With ThePrime, you get professional support without the overhead. No payroll taxes, no benefits, no office space, no training costs, and no long-term commitment. You also get a team (not just one person), so there's always coverage even if someone is sick or on vacation. Plus, you can scale up or down as needed.
All of our virtual assistants are based in the United States. We believe in providing native English speakers who understand American business culture and can represent your brand professionally.
